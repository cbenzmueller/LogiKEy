\title{When We Disagree to Agree:\\
  An Isabelle/HOL Formalization of Implicit Disagreement
  in Abstract Argumentation}
\author{Luca Pasetto \and Christoph Benzm\"uller}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
  This document presents an Isabelle/HOL formalization of agreement,
  explanation, and implicit disagreement in Dung-style abstract
  argumentation, accompanying the paper \emph{When We Disagree to Agree: An
  Abstract Argumentation Account of Implicit Disagreement in Legal
  Reasoning} (JURIX 2026 submission). The development builds on a corrected
  version of the higher-order-logic encoding of abstract argumentation by
  Fuenmayor and Steen (session \texttt{AA\_Fixed}). It covers agents and
  coalitions as induced subframeworks, three inference relations (credulous,
  shared conclusion, shared argument), proximal and complete explanations,
  shared-explanation inference, ordinary, proximal, and distal (implicit)
  disagreement, weak spots, and antagonists. All definitions are stated for
  arbitrary, possibly infinite frameworks. The example theories formalize the
  paper's running six-argument example graph and machine-check its computed
  properties---preferred extensions, weak spots, and antagonists---together
  with the coalition-only distal-disagreement example.
\end{abstract}
