\begin{isamarkuptext}%
The object language of the axiomatic semantics of Fatio
\cite{McBurneyParsons2005}, as formalised in \cite{PasettoBenzmueller2024}: agent beliefs \isa{{\isasymB}\isactrlsup d} and
desires \isa{{\isasymD}\isactrlsup d} over the content logic, with three further kinds of atoms: \isa{Done\isactrlsup d}
for uttered locutions, \isa{EntD} for argumentative entailment, and \isa{ExJD\ i\ {\isasymphi}} whose
single semantic clause carries the existential of the pre-conditions of question
and challenge, \isa{{\isacharparenleft}{\kern0pt}{\isasymexists}{\isasymDelta}{\isacharparenright}{\kern0pt}\ Done{\isacharbrackleft}{\kern0pt}justify{\isacharparenleft}{\kern0pt}i{\isacharcomma}{\kern0pt}{\isasymDelta}{\isasymturnstile}\isactrlsup s\isactrlsup g{\isasymphi}{\isacharparenright}{\kern0pt}{\isacharbrackright}{\kern0pt}}, inside the object language. Following
the sign-parametric justify locution of the LogiKEy sources \cite{LogiKEy2020}, \isa{ExJD} carries the
sign of the requested justification.%
